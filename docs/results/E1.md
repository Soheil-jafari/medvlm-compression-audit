## Reading

Rule 3 passes: the teacher's 0.803 is at least E1 seed 0's 0.592 + 0.02. The students answer
"yes" very rarely: they find a third of the polyps and almost none of the ulcerative colitis or
oesophagitis, with specificity close to 1. Their scores still rank polyp and oesophagitis well
(AUROC 0.995 and about 0.93), so most of the low sensitivity is a cautious threshold; for
ulcerative colitis the AUROC is low too (about 0.6), so that finding is also poorly
discriminated. This led to the operating point at specificity 0.95 (pre-registration section 11
item 10). The image reliance is weaker than the teacher's: the blank-image drop is below Gate
1's 0.10 for every seed (Gate 1 applies to the teacher only), and the lesion-mask effect is
smaller (0.09-0.28, intervals above 0). Seed 0 logged a NaN final loss (RC-E1 section 3b); its
validation is the best of the three.
