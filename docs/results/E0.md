## Reading

Gate 1 passes on every check: balanced accuracy 0.80 (threshold 0.65), blank-image drop 0.30
(0.10), and the lesion-mask effect 0.36 with its interval above zero (masking the polyp flips
73 % of the answers, an area-matched random region 37 %). The teacher is conservative: its
specificity is about 1.0, but it finds only 68 % of the polyps, 46 % of the ulcerative colitis
and 70 % of the oesophagitis. It is well calibrated (ECE 0.026). Whether the low
sensitivity is only a cautious threshold or also weak discrimination is read from the
per-finding AUROC (pre-registration section 11 item 9); E0's comes with its next evaluation
(RC-R1 on the test splits), because the saved validation scores were lost. Only the 227 validation
images with an abnormality answer are probed (`n_probes_scored` 681 = 227 x 3), so a
finding's negatives are images with another abnormality, never normal mucosa. Only 13 of the
86 masked validation polyps are small (below the training q1), so the small-finding recall
has a wide interval (0.50-1.00). The 14 image-reliance rows of Gate 1 were replaced in the
file by the validation pass's rows of the same names (the same tests and seed); the three
Gate 1 checks hold on the committed values. Rule 3's quality part (E0 at least E1 seed 0 +
0.02 in macro balanced accuracy) is read once RC-E1 seed 0 is committed.
