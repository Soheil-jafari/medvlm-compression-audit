## Reading

- E2 is as cautious as E1 with its plain answers: specificity 1.00, sensitivity low, a macro
  balanced accuracy (0.553) inside E1's range of seeds. Its scores rank the findings at least
  as well as E1's: the ulcerative colitis AUROC (0.80) is above all three E1 seeds (0.58-0.67),
  polyp and oesophagitis are close to E1's. One seed, so this is not a result yet.
- **The operating point is fitted on these same validation probes** (specificity 0.95 on the
  validation negatives, pre-registration section 11 item 10), so the `op_*` numbers above are
  in-sample: specificity is about 0.95 by construction, and the sensitivities are optimistic.
  The test split reads these thresholds unchanged.
- The small-polyp recall at the operating point is 13 of 13; the bootstrap cannot vary a
  proportion that is 1 in every resample, so its interval (1.00-1.00) is degenerate and says
  nothing about the uncertainty.
- `lesion_mask_effect` (0.058, interval 0.012-0.116, above 0) is measured at the **default**
  threshold (the plain answers), where E2 says "yes" on only 21 % of the masked polyps
  (`original_yes_rate` 0.209). A flip is any change of answer, but the change masking a
  polyp should cause is a "yes" turning into a "no", and a model that says "no" to almost every
  masked polyp already has little room for it. The effect is smaller than E1's (0.09-0.28), but
  E2 also starts from fewer "yes" answers (E1 0.30-0.52), so the two are not comparable as
  they stand.
- No E2-minus-E1 claim at the operating point is made until E1's `op_*` rows exist (RC-E1
  section 3c).
- Training: 16 of 625 updates skipped on a non-finite micro-batch loss (RC-E2 section 6b);
  the adapter is finite. Training took 1.9 times as long as E1's (27.2 s per update).
