# Final goal: the results an article needs

This file defines **done**. The project is finished when every box below is ticked,
each tick points to a file in this repository, and all three team members have signed
off at the bottom. The research loop (`docs/agent/PROTOCOL.md`) works towards this
list and never claims a box without the evidence it names.

The goal is **complete, honest evidence**, not a particular outcome. If the clinical
criterion does not beat generic pruning, the article becomes an audit paper with a
reported negative result (shape B below). Both shapes are acceptable end states.

## 1. The article

| | Shape A: method + audit | Shape B: audit + negative result |
|---|---|---|
| When | Gate 2 passes and H3 is supported on the test split | H3 is not supported or is inconclusive |
| Lead claim | A clinically weighted pruning criterion preserves small and rare findings better than generic criteria at the same measured budget | At a matched budget, compression families differ in what they forget, and average accuracy hides it; a clinically targeted criterion is not enough on its own |
| Contributions | C1 audit, C2 criterion, C3 harness | C1 audit, C3 harness, C2 reported as a tested hypothesis |

Venue is open (`docs/decisions_log.md`). Unless the team decides otherwise, the
draft targets a full conference paper of about 8 pages plus appendix and can be
extended to a journal version.

Research questions and hypotheses (from the project definition):

| | Question | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | At a matched budget, which compression family damages small-finding recall, rare-finding recall, image reliance and calibration most? | **H1** damage to small and rare findings is larger than damage to average accuracy, for every family. **H2** the families differ: structured pruning harms small findings more than 8-bit quantization. |
| RQ2 | Does a clinically weighted pruning criterion preserve small and rare findings better than generic criteria at the same budget? | **H3** the clinical criterion (E7) gives higher small-finding recall and a larger lesion-mask effect than the best of E5/E6 at B2. |
| RQ3 | Is headline accuracy a reliable guide to clinical safety after compression? | **H4** ranking compressed models by accuracy gives a different order than ranking them by small-finding recall or calibration. |

## 2. Hypothesis verdicts

Each verdict is one of **supported / not supported / inconclusive**, stated with the
effect size and 95% CI, and is written into `docs/results/VERDICTS.md` by the
aggregation step, not by hand.

- [ ] **H1** for each of E3, E4, E5, E6, E7, E8 at each budget: relative drop vs E2 in
  `small_finding_recall` and rare-group recall compared with the relative drop in
  overall accuracy; paired bootstrap CI on the difference of drops.
- [ ] **H2** at matched memory: small-finding recall of the best pruning arm (E5/E6)
  vs E3 `w8`; paired bootstrap CI and McNemar.
- [ ] **H3 (primary)** at B2: E7 minus the pre-registered best of E5/E6 on
  `small_finding_recall` and `lesion_mask_effect`; paired bootstrap 95% CIs; Holm
  correction across the two endpoints; 3 seeds.
- [ ] **H4** Kendall's tau between the ranking of all compressed arms by accuracy and
  by small-finding recall, rare recall and ECE, with a bootstrap CI.

## 4. Quality bars

- [ ] Pre-registration frozen **before** the first compression run (E3-E9, A1-A6);
  freeze commit hash recorded in `docs/preregistration.md`; deviations listed.
- [ ] The student was chosen by the frozen D05 protocol on validation data only,
  before any compression run; all rounds are reported.
- [ ] Every training arm has 3 seeds; every run has a completed run card in
  `docs/runs/` with the commit it ran from.
- [ ] All budget-matched arms are within the pre-registered tolerance of B1/B2/B3,
  checked by a test over `results/`.
- [ ] Recovery is identical for every pruning arm (`tests/test_configs.py`).
- [ ] Pruned units are physically removed; latency and peak memory are measured on
  the timing GPU, not estimated; low-precision kernels are confirmed to run.
- [ ] Patient-level splits verified disjoint; external test deduplicated against
  training images, with the removed count in its data card.
- [ ] Finding weights and size thresholds were not changed after any test result
  (decisions log shows no post-freeze change, or a logged and approved deviation).
- [ ] Statistics as pre-registered: paired bootstrap 95% CIs, McNemar per finding,
  Holm correction.
- [ ] No number in `paper/sections/*.tex` is typed by hand: results sections only
  `\input` generated tables or use macros from `numbers.tex` (checked by a test).

## 7. Stop conditions

- **Done:** sections 2-6 all ticked and signed off below. The loop sets
  `docs/agent/STATUS.md` to `DONE` and stops doing work.
- **Pivot needed:** Gate 1 fails (the teacher does not use the image, or damage is
  not visible) for the chosen pair. The loop stops at that point and asks the team
  to choose a new pair, task or domain. It never pivots on its own.
- **Shape B:** if H3 is not supported on the test split, the loop continues to
  completion with shape B. It does not add new criteria or re-tune weights to rescue
  H3.
